# ⚖️ Decisions Modality

Evaluating conditions, acting as gatekeepers, and performing strict classification or routing logic.

Primarily utilized by the Center Server for the [Central Gatekeeper Flow](../system/gatekeeper-flow.md), this modality relies on fast, deterministic models to approve or reject actions.
